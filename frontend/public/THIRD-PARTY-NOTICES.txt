AppInsight brand mark
=====================

The AppInsight brand mark is based on the Tabler Icons "chart-dots" icon:
https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/icons/outline/chart-dots.svg

Tabler Icons is distributed under the MIT License:
https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/LICENSE

The source geometry is adapted for AppInsight with the project's brand colors.
